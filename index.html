What your licence covers

A summary of the main terms. The full terms are set out in the licence agreement you sign.

Included

  • Selling the courses under your own brand, at your own prices
  • Renaming the courses and editing the learning materials
  • Hosting them on your own website or learning platform
  • Using them within your existing training programmes
  • Putting them forward to your own accreditation provider
  • Enrolling as many learners as you like, with no extra fees

Not included

  • Reselling or transferring the licence or the PLR rights
  • Listing the courses on Udemy, Skillshare, Coursera, Uthena or YouTube
  • Sole rights: the licence is non-exclusive, so other partner academies may license the same courses

Questions academies ask

Can't find your answer? Email business@janets.org.uk and we'll get back to you.

What exactly do I receive?

The video lessons and accompanying learning materials for each course you license. We confirm the full file list in your licence agreement before you pay.

Can I see a sample lesson first?

Yes. for the course you're most interested in and we'll send a sample, so you can judge the quality for your students.

What makes the five courses exclusive?

They're newly produced and haven't been published anywhere else. They're only available to academies that license them from us, either on their own or in the full bundle, so your students won't find them on other course sites.

How long are the courses, and what language are they in?

The core courses run from about one to seven hours each, over 40 hours in total. Every lesson and its running time is listed in the collection above. All courses are in English.

Are the courses accredited?

The licence covers the course content, not a qualification, and the courses are not Ofqual-regulated qualifications. You're welcome to put them forward to your own accreditation or CPD provider and issue certificates under your academy's name.

Are the treatment courses practical training?

They are online, video-based courses. They work well as the theory and knowledge part of your training, alongside the hands-on practice and assessment you already run.

Can I change the courses?

Yes. You can rename the courses, add your branding and edit the learning materials so they fit your programmes.

How do I pay, and are there ongoing costs?

A single one-time payment. There are no renewals, subscriptions or per-learner fees. Payment details are set out in your licence agreement.

I don't have a learning platform yet. Can you help?

Yes. We can set up and host a learning platform for you. Ask us about it in your meeting.

Do you work with academies outside the UK?

Yes. We license the collection to beauty academies and training providers internationally.

Are there other ways to work together?

Yes. Alongside PLR, we run a partner programme that lets you earn from courses beyond the ones you license, with very little to manage. We'll walk you through it in your meeting.
